# Sources, rights, and provenance

## Evidence for this public record

A curated Nov 4, 2026 local investigation used:

- Six private Markdown research reports based on source files, logs, benchmarks and experiment reports.
- An archived four-panel board of October 3, October 4 and October 16 scene captures.
- The creator's account of the relationship to the later UE5 project.

Private internal file paths, original evidence archive, AI conversation transcripts, private URLs, and full source dumps are intentionally omitted.

## Confirmed and unconfirmed

- The images are archived captures rather than newly recreated historical footage.
- Image dates are inferred from reports and file timestamps, **not independently verified camera metadata**.
- The initial launch capture and exact first work date were not found.
- Screenshots have different viewpoints and development stages, so they are **not a controlled graphics benchmark**.
- The UE5 forest was separately implemented, but its full development history has not been audited here.

## Asset and license rights

- Three.js and related open-source components have their respective licensing requirements.
- PBR materials and vegetation assets used in the private forest project can have different rights.
- This repository distributes **no game source, shaders, meshes, textures, audio or original asset libraries**.
- Original Markdown text is MIT licensed within the defined [license scope](../LICENSE_SCOPE.md). This does **not** grant rights over screenshots, the objects depicted in them, or other people's works.
